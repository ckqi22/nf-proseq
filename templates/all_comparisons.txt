Legend:
A: Gene identifier
B: FoldChange value, in up sheet, the value is greater than 1, in down sheet, the value is less than 1
C: The logarithmic transformation (base 2) of the fold change values, positive values denote up-regulation, negative values denote down-regulation
D: Statistical significance of the differential expression
E: False Discovery Rate
{count_range}: Raw Read Counts
{fpkm_range}: Normalized Expression (FPKM), gene expression normalized as Fragments Per Kilobase of gene per Million mapped reads, accounting for both gene length and sequencing depth.
{cpm_range}: Normalized Expression (CPM), Counts Per Million reads mapped, normalized for sequencing depth only.
{annotation_range}: Gene annotation, functional annotations or metadata associated with each gene
